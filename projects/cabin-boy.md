## What it is

Cabin Boy is a game I made with my teammate **Ben Harper**: a hand-painted pirate adventure starring the little bandana-wearing kid in the key art above. We took it from idea to a playable build and brought it to the floor at the **XP Gaming conference in PEI** (#XP25), where attendees could sit down and actually play it.

## The win

Cabin Boy won **Community Choice** at the conference, voted for by the people who came by the booth and played it. That's the part I care about most: the award came from players, not a rubric. The feedback was good enough that we walked away itching to jump straight back into development.

## What's next

I'm reposting the game on my own itch.io so anyone can play it. More to come.
