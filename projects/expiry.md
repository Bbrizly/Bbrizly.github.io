## What it is

Expiry is a kitchen-side app built to attack one specific, expensive problem: restaurants throw out food that quietly expires in the back of the walk-in. The idea was to make spoilage visible before it happens and turn it into a daily action instead of a monthly write-off.

## How it works

- **Scan expiry dates with the camera.** Point it at a package's printed date, confirm the ingredient, and it goes into inventory. No manual data entry.
- **Track inventory by shipment.** Every ingredient shows its category, where it lives (fridge, dry), and how many days until it turns, color-coded by urgency. Filter to High Risk or Expiring < 2 days.
- **See which dishes are at risk.** The home view lists the restaurant's dishes with their ingredients tagged by how soon each one expires, so the kitchen knows what to cook down first.

## Context

Built end to end inside the **UPEI Hackathon** window: concept, build, and demo within the event.
