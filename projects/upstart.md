## What it was

Upstart was a SaaS platform aimed at automating the busywork that runs a company, with marketing as the flagship product. The pitch was simple: a small team should be able to market like a full department without hiring one.

You would give Upstart a goal (a launch, a campaign, an awareness push) and it would plan a full **marketing sprint** end to end:

- **Generate** the content set: posts, captions, threads, and creatives tuned per channel.
- **Schedule** the whole sprint on an optimal cadence instead of one post at a time.
- **Auto-post** across LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and TikTok, then track reach and engagement back into the dashboard.

The dashboard above is the command center: live campaign performance, an auto-scheduled queue, and active sprints running on autopilot.
