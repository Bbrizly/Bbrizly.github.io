How do I add a book?

Add an EPUB or PDF from Files, or share one straight into the app. It is copied into your library, so moving the original never breaks the book. Every book keeps its own place, down to the word.

02

How does word-level highlighting work?

Pick any voice on your phone and press play. The highlight follows the narration exactly, at 0.75x through 2.0x, and it keeps going with the screen off, with the book on your lock screen.

03

What is RSVP speed reading?

Speed reading holds a single word in a fixed spot so your eyes stop hunting. 100 to 1000 words a minute, with the sentence underneath when you need the context back.

04

What is a recall prompt?

A recall card interrupts on purpose. Say or type what happened, and it checks your answer against the passage you already finished. It never reads ahead, so nothing gets spoiled.

05

Can I ask the book a question?

Lost track of a character? Ask. The answer is built only from the part you have already read. On device by default, so the question stays with you.

What is Inside Voice?

Inside Voice is a privacy-first iOS 18+ reader for EPUB and PDF files on iPhone and iPad. It narrates your books with word-level highlighting synced to speech, offers RSVP speed reading from 100 to 1000 words per minute, and interrupts with recall prompts so you summarize a passage before you see it again. Book chat answers only from the part you have already read, so spoilers stay blocked.

There is no account, no ads, and no analytics. Books, rendered audio, and reading position stay in the app's own storage unless you turn on an optional cloud AI key for Gemini or Azure. The app is free and heading for the App Store.

How is Inside Voice different from Voice Dream or ElevenReader?

Voice Dream and ElevenReader are strong at turning documents into spoken audio. Inside Voice is built around one chapter timeline: every word has a start and end time, so highlighting, RSVP speed reading, and narration cannot drift apart. Scrubbing is a lookup on that list, not a guess.

It also adds recall prompts and book chat that never read ahead of your place, and it ships without an account or analytics. Optional neural and system voices run on device after any one-time downloads you choose. Cloud AI is bring-your-own-key only.

Does Inside Voice work offline?

Yes for reading aloud with iOS system voices, including enhanced and premium voices you install on the phone. Background playback and lock-screen controls keep working with no signal. An optional neural voice downloads once, then runs entirely on device.

Recall and book chat default to on-device Apple Intelligence when your device and region support it. Gemini or Azure only run if you paste your own keys. Books never need a server to open or keep your place.

What is the recall prompt?

A recall card stops you on purpose after a stretch of reading and asks what just happened. You can speak or type an answer. The app checks it against a short summary of the paragraphs behind your playhead, never against text you have not reached yet.

That pattern is retrieval practice: saying what you remember before you reread. On-device is the default. Optional cloud models use only the passage you already finished, and only with a key you supply.
